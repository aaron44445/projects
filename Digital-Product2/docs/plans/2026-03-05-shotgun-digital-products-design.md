# Shotgun Digital Products — Design Document

**Date:** 2026-03-05
**Goal:** Cash flow ASAP through rapid digital product launches
**Strategy:** Build 5-8 small digital products fast, promote each via short-form video personal brand, double down on winners

---

## Core Strategy

Build a personal brand as an AI builder on TikTok/Reels/Shorts. The audience follows the journey of someone building AI-powered products. The products are monetized through that audience. Each product built = content. Content = audience. Audience = sales.

---

## Product Lineup (8 Products)

### Tier 1 — Fastest to Build, Proven Demand

1. **AI Prompt Packs** — Curated prompt collections for specific outcomes (business, content creation, coding). Price: $7-15.
2. **Aesthetic Wallpaper + Widget Packs** — Dark mode productivity wallpapers, phone/desktop setups tied to "locked in" grindset aesthetic. Price: $5-9.
3. **"Locked In" Notion/Productivity Template Pack** — Goal tracking, habit systems, daily planners. Price: $9-19.

### Tier 2 — Slightly More Build Time, Higher Value

4. **"Build in Public" Content Kit** — Templates for threads, TikTok scripts, carousel formats, 30-day content calendar for people documenting their journey. Price: $15-25.
5. **Landing Page Templates** — Beautiful, ready-to-deploy single-page sites for indie products. HTML or Next.js. Price: $19-39.
6. **"Solo Founder Dashboard"** — Single-page web app: daily command center with goals, habits, finances. Free tier for traffic, paid tier for revenue. Price: $19-29 one-time or $5/mo.

### Tier 3 — Higher Effort, Bigger Potential

7. **"The AI Business Blueprint"** — Mini digital guide on using AI agents to run parts of a business. Price: $29-49.
8. **Micro SaaS Tool** — Tiny single-purpose web tool with free tier and paid unlock. Price: $5-9/mo.

## Marketing Engine

### Personal Brand Angle

"Watch me build AI products and make money from them." The brand is the person, the products are what's sold through the audience.

### Content Formula Per Product (3-5 videos each)

- **The Hook Video** — "I built [product] in [timeframe] and here's what happened" — screen recording, sped up, music
- **The Result Video** — Finished product in action, before/after for templates, satisfying UI for tools
- **The Why Video** — Short talking-head or text overlay explaining the problem solved
- **The Social Proof Video** — Sales notifications, reviews, reactions
- **The Behind-the-Scenes** — "Day in my life building digital products"

### Posting Strategy

- Platform priority: TikTok first, cross-post to Reels and Shorts
- Frequency: 1-2 videos per day
- Link in bio: Linktree-style page listing all products
- Niches: #buildinpublic #digitalproducts #sidehustle #productivitytok #indiehacker

## Tech Stack

### Sales Platforms

- **Start:** Gumroad (zero friction, built-in discovery)
- **Scale:** Move to Payhip at ~$2K/month (lower fees)
- **Web apps:** Deploy on Vercel (free tier), paywall with Stripe or Lemon Squeezy

## Build Order

| Week | Products | Rationale |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Week 1 | Prompt Pack + Wallpaper Pack | Fastest to create, zero code, instant listing |
| Week 1-2 | Notion Template Pack | High demand, great TikTok content |

| Week 2-3 | Landing Page Templates | Leverages existing frontend skills |

---

## Pricing Strategy

- Start cheap ($5-19) to get volume and reviews
- Raise prices or bundle into "Complete Stack" ($49-79) once social proof exists
- Free lead magnet (1 free template or 5 free prompts) to build email list

---

## Success Metrics

- Which TikToks get views (audience interest signal)
- Which products get link-in-bio clicks (conversion signal)
- After 2-3 weeks: identify 1-2 winners and go deeper on those
