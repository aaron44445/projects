# 30-Day Build-in-Public Content Calendar

A complete 30-day content plan that mixes platforms, content types, and templates from this kit. Follow it as-is or adapt to your schedule. Each day maps to a specific template from the kit for easy execution.

**How to use this calendar:**
1. Fill in the template blanks with your own project details
2. Batch-create content 3-5 days ahead when possible
3. Every 7th day is a rest/repost day — use it to repost your best performer or take a break
4. Adjust post times to your audience's timezone

## Tips for Execution

**Batching:** On Sunday evening, prep the next 5 days of content. Fill in the blanks for each template, write captions, and schedule what you can.

**Repurposing:** Every thread can become a carousel. Every TikTok script can become a Twitter thread. If something performs well on one platform, adapt it for another.

**Engagement windows:** After posting, spend 15-30 minutes engaging with replies and commenting on others' posts. The algorithm rewards early engagement.

**Tracking:** Keep a simple spreadsheet tracking each post's impressions, engagement, and any direct revenue. After 30 days, double down on what worked.

**Consistency over virality:** The goal is not to go viral. The goal is to show up every day, build trust, and let compounding do the work. One month of consistent posting beats one viral hit.
