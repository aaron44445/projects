# Carousel Templates

8 fill-in-the-blank carousel formats for Instagram and LinkedIn. Each carousel has a cover slide, content slides, and a CTA slide. Design each slide with bold text, minimal clutter, and high contrast.

---

## 1. Step-by-Step Tutorial

**Platform:** Instagram + LinkedIn

**Slide 1 (Cover):**
*Headline:* "How to [ACHIEVE RESULT] in [NUMBER] Steps"
*Subtext:* "A step-by-step guide for [TARGET AUDIENCE]"
*Visual:* Bold headline text centered on a solid dark background. Accent color underline on the key phrase. Small arrow icon or "Swipe >" indicator in bottom right.

**Slide 2:**
*Header:* "Step 1: [STEP NAME]"
*Body:*
"[INSTRUCTION — e.g., Open [TOOL NAME] and create a new [THING]. The key here is to [SPECIFIC TIP]. Don't skip this — it sets up everything that comes next.]"
*Visual tip:* Screenshot or icon illustrating the step.

**Slide 3:**
*Header:* "Step 2: [STEP NAME]"
*Body:*
"[INSTRUCTION — e.g., Now take your [THING FROM STEP 1] and [ACTION]. The mistake most people make here is [COMMON MISTAKE]. Instead, do [BETTER APPROACH].]"

**Slide 4:**
*Header:* "Step 3: [STEP NAME]"
*Body:*
"[INSTRUCTION — e.g., This is where it comes together. [ACTION] using [METHOD/TOOL]. You should now have [RESULT OF THIS STEP].]"

**Slide 5:**
*Header:* "Step 4: [STEP NAME]"
*Body:*
"[INSTRUCTION — e.g., The final step is [ACTION]. Most people over-complicate this. Keep it simple: [SIMPLIFIED APPROACH]. Done is better than perfect.]"

**Slide 6:**
*Header:* "The Result"
*Body:*
"Following these 4 steps, you'll have [END RESULT — e.g., a fully launched product / a complete content calendar / your first paying customer]."
*Visual tip:* Before/after comparison or final outcome screenshot.

**Slide 7 (CTA):**
*Headline:* "Want the full guide?"
*Body:*
"Save this post for later. Follow @[YOUR HANDLE] for more step-by-step builds. Link in bio for [PRODUCT/RESOURCE]."

## 2. Before/After Transformation

**Platform:** Instagram + LinkedIn

**Slide 1 (Cover):**
*Headline:* "[TOPIC]: Before vs. After"
*Subtext:* "What [TIMEFRAME/ACTION] of [EFFORT] actually looks like"
*Visual:* Split design — left side dark/muted (Before), right side bright/accent color (After).

**Slide 2:**
*Header:* "BEFORE: [BEFORE STATE]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIBE THE BEFORE — e.g., I was [SITUATION]. My [METRIC] was [BAD NUMBER]. I spent [TIME] doing [INEFFICIENT THING]. Every day felt like [NEGATIVE FEELING].]"
*Visual tip:* Muted colors, simple/plain design to convey the "before" feeling.

**Slide 3:**
*Header:* "The turning point"
*Body:*
"What changed: [THE CATALYST — e.g., I discovered [THING/METHOD/TOOL] and decided to commit to [ACTION] for [TIMEFRAME]. The first week was [HONEST DESCRIPTION]. But I kept going.]"

**Slide 4:**
*Header:* "AFTER: [AFTER STATE]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIBE THE AFTER — e.g., Now my [METRIC] is [GOOD NUMBER]. I spend [LESS TIME] on [TASK] because [REASON]. The biggest difference is [QUALITATIVE CHANGE].]"
*Visual tip:* Bright colors, accent highlights to convey the "after" energy.

**Slide 5:**
*Header:* "What actually made the difference"
*Body:*
"It wasn't [COMMON ASSUMPTION — e.g., working harder / a magic tool / some secret hack].

It was:
1. [REAL FACTOR — e.g., Being consistent for 90 days without expecting results]
2. [REAL FACTOR — e.g., Cutting out everything that wasn't moving the needle]
3. [REAL FACTOR — e.g., Asking for help instead of figuring it all out alone]"

**Slide 6 (CTA):**
*Headline:* "Your transformation starts today"
*Body:*
"Save this as a reminder. Follow @[YOUR HANDLE] for more honest build-in-public content. Drop a comment if you're working on your own transformation."

## 4. Tool/Product Comparison

**Platform:** LinkedIn + Instagram

**Slide 1 (Cover):**
*Headline:* "[TOOL A] vs [TOOL B] for [USE CASE]"
*Subtext:* "An honest comparison from someone who's used both"
*Visual:* Two logos or icons side by side with "VS" in the center.

**Slide 2:**
*Header:* "[TOOL A] — The Good"
*Body:*
"Best for: [USE CASE]
Key strengths:
- [STRENGTH 1 — e.g., Dead-simple setup, takes 5 minutes]
- [STRENGTH 2 — e.g., Great free tier for beginners]
- [STRENGTH 3 — e.g., Built-in analytics]
Price: $[AMOUNT]/month"

**Slide 3:**
*Header:* "[TOOL A] — The Bad"
*Body:*
"Where it falls short:
- [WEAKNESS 1 — e.g., Limited customization options]
- [WEAKNESS 2 — e.g., Gets expensive as you scale]
- [WEAKNESS 3 — e.g., Customer support is slow]"

**Slide 4:**
*Header:* "[TOOL B] — The Good"
*Body:*
"Best for: [USE CASE]
Key strengths:
- [STRENGTH 1 — e.g., Highly customizable]
- [STRENGTH 2 — e.g., Better pricing at scale]
- [STRENGTH 3 — e.g., More integrations]
Price: $[AMOUNT]/month"

**Slide 5:**
*Header:* "[TOOL B] — The Bad"
*Body:*
"Where it falls short:
- [WEAKNESS 1 — e.g., Steeper learning curve]
- [WEAKNESS 2 — e.g., Setup takes longer]
- [WEAKNESS 3 — e.g., UI feels dated]"

**Slide 6:**
*Header:* "My recommendation"
*Body:*
"Choose [TOOL A] if: [SCENARIO — e.g., you're just starting out and want zero friction]
Choose [TOOL B] if: [SCENARIO — e.g., you need more control and are willing to invest setup time]

I personally use [YOUR CHOICE] because [REASON]."

**Slide 7 (CTA):**
*Headline:* "Which one do you use?"
*Body:*
"Comment your pick below. Save this for your next tool decision. Follow @[YOUR HANDLE] for more honest tool reviews."

## 5. "Day in My Life as a [Role]"

**Platform:** Instagram

**Slide 1 (Cover):**
*Headline:* "A Day in My Life as a [ROLE — e.g., Solo Founder / Indie Hacker / Creator]"
*Subtext:* "Building [PROJECT NAME] in public"
*Visual:* Lifestyle photo or workspace shot. Clean overlay text.

**Slide 2:**
*Header:* "[TIME — e.g., 6:30 AM] — [ACTIVITY]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIPTION — e.g., Wake up, no alarm. First thing I do is [MORNING ROUTINE — e.g., review yesterday's metrics with coffee]. I check [WHAT YOU CHECK — e.g., revenue dashboard, overnight emails, social notifications].]"

**Slide 3:**
*Header:* "[TIME — e.g., 8:00 AM] — [ACTIVITY — e.g., Deep work block]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIPTION — e.g., This is my most productive time. Phone on DND. I spend this block on [HIGHEST-PRIORITY TASK — e.g., building the core product / writing content]. No meetings, no email.]"

**Slide 4:**
*Header:* "[TIME — e.g., 11:00 AM] — [ACTIVITY — e.g., Marketing & content]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIPTION — e.g., I switch to distribution. Today I'm [TASK — e.g., writing a Twitter thread about yesterday's milestone / editing a TikTok / responding to DMs from potential customers].]"

**Slide 5:**
*Header:* "[TIME — e.g., 1:00 PM] — [ACTIVITY — e.g., Break + admin]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIPTION — e.g., Lunch break, then I handle [ADMIN TASKS — e.g., emails, invoices, customer support]. I batch these so they don't eat my morning. This takes about [TIME].]"

**Slide 6:**
*Header:* "[TIME — e.g., 3:00 PM] — [ACTIVITY — e.g., Afternoon build session]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIPTION — e.g., Second focus block. I work on [SECONDARY PRIORITY — e.g., new feature / next product / email sequences]. Energy is lower so I pick tasks that need less creativity.]"

**Slide 7:**
*Header:* "[TIME — e.g., 6:00 PM] — [ACTIVITY — e.g., Wrap up + plan tomorrow]"
*Body:*
"[DESCRIPTION — e.g., I review what I shipped today, write tomorrow's task list (3 items max), and post my daily build update. Then I close the laptop. Hard stop.]"

**Slide 8 (CTA):**
*Headline:* "That's the real day. No glamour."
*Body:*
"Follow @[YOUR HANDLE] to see the daily build. What does your day look like? Comment below."
