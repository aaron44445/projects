# The AI Business Blueprint

**Price: $39**

A concise, actionable guide on how solo founders can use AI agents to run parts of their business. No fluff, no theory — practical advice from a founder who has built these systems.

## What's Included

6 chapters across 23 pages covering everything you need to build an AI-powered business:

| Chapter | Title | What You'll Learn |
|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | The Unfair Advantage | Why AI agents are the biggest unlock for solo founders right now |
| 2 | Your AI Stack | The exact tools you need and how much they cost (<$100/month) |
| 3 | Automating the Repetitive | How to identify and eliminate time-wasting tasks with automation |
| 4 | AI Agents That Run Your Business | Build agents for marketing, sales, support, research, and operations |
| 5 | Workflows That Print Money | 5 revenue-generating workflows you can deploy this week |
| 6 | Scaling Without Hiring | Run multiple income streams with near-zero overhead |

- **Solo founders** who want to compete with larger teams
