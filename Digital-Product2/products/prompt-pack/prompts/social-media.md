# Social Media Prompts

## 1. TikTok Script Machine

**The Prompt:**

> You are a TikTok script writer who understands that people scroll past 90% of videos in 1.5 seconds.
>
> Write me a 60-second TikTok script about: [TOPIC]
>
> MANDATORY STRUCTURE:
> - **Hook (0-2 sec)**: Pattern interrupt. Not a question. Not "Have you ever..." Start mid-action, mid-conflict, or with something visually shocking.
> - **Body (3-45 sec)**: Fast cuts. One idea per 3-second segment. [VISUAL CUE] markers for when to show something, cut, or switch angles.
> - **Payoff (46-55 sec)**: Deliver the value. No fluff.
> - **CTA (56-60 sec)**: What to do next. Make it specific, not "follow for more."
>
> Write it the way people actually TALK in videos, not the way people write. Use sentence fragments. Interruptions. Emphasis on random words. That's how humans speak on camera.
>
> Give me 3 hook variations to test — I'll pick the strongest one.

**When to use it:** When you need a scroll-stopping TikTok script that sounds natural on camera, not stiff and scripted.

**Pro tip:** Record all 3 hooks and test them as separate videos — hooks make or break performance, not the content after.

## 2. Twitter/X Thread Builder

**The Prompt:**

> You are a Twitter thread architect who knows that threads live or die by the first tweet.
>
> Build me a thread about: [TOPIC]
>
> RULES:
> 1. **Tweet 1** must work as a standalone banger — 90% of people won't click "Show more." Make it provocative, specific, or contrarian. No setup tweets.
> 2. **Tweets 2-8** deliver on the promise. One clear idea per tweet. No filler. If you're saying "It's important to note" you're wasting characters.
> 3. **Final tweet** = action step, not "If you liked this, follow me." Tell me what to do with this information.
> 4. Each tweet must be under 280 characters but feel complete — no cliffhanger gimmicks.
>
> Show me the thread with tweet numbers and character counts. Then give me 2 alternative options for tweet 1 in case the first one doesn't hit.

**When to use it:** When you need a high-performing thread that doesn't rely on engagement bait to get reads.

**Pro tip:** Post tweet 1 solo first and gauge response for 10 minutes — if it flops, try an alternate hook before threading.

## 3. Instagram Carousel Creator

**The Prompt:**

> You are an Instagram carousel designer who understands that slide 1 is an ad for slide 2.
>
> Create a carousel about: [TOPIC]
>
> SLIDE STRUCTURE (design it for me):
> - **Slide 1**: Bold claim or question + "Swipe to see why →" (but phrase it better than that)
> - **Slides 2-7**: One insight per slide. Hierarchy: Big idea at top, explanation below, visual suggestion in [BRACKETS].
> - **Slide 8**: Summary or action step + CTA
>
> For each slide, write:
> 1. **Headline** (the big text people see while scrolling)
> 2. **Body copy** (supporting text, 1-2 lines max)
> 3. **[VISUAL SUGGESTION]** (what image, icon, or design element supports this slide)
>
> Also write the caption: start with a hook (not "In this carousel..."), add value in 3-5 lines, end with a question to drive comments.

**When to use it:** When you need a swipe-worthy carousel that actually teaches something instead of restating the obvious.

**Pro tip:** Slide 1 performance predicts everything — if people don't swipe, the rest doesn't matter. Test different slide 1 headlines.

## 4. LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post

**The Prompt:**

> You are a LinkedIn ghostwriter for executives who don't want to sound like robots.
>
> Write me a LinkedIn post about: [TOPIC]
>
> STRUCTURE:
> 1. **Opening line**: Personal story, contrarian observation, or bold claim. Not "I've been thinking about..." — start with impact.
> 2. **Body (3-5 short paragraphs)**: Share the lesson, framework, or insight. Use line breaks liberally. One idea per paragraph. No buzzword salad.
> 3. **Closing**: Takeaway + conversation starter (ask something specific, not "What do you think?").
>
> TONE: Professional but human. Like you're talking to a smart colleague over coffee, not presenting at a board meeting. Use "I" and "you." Share what you actually learned, not what sounds impressive.
>
> NO: "Thrilled to announce," "I'm humbled," "Lessons learned," "Game changer," "Excited to share."
>
> Format with short paragraphs and line breaks — LinkedIn rewards readability.

**When to use it:** When you want to build authority without sounding like a corporate press release or motivation poster.

**Pro tip:** The posts that perform best on LinkedIn tell a story about failure, change, or an unconventional approach — not your wins.

## 5. Engagement Hook Library

**The Prompt:**

> You are a hook specialist who knows that 90% of content fails because the first sentence is boring.
>
> Generate 20 high-performing hooks for content about: [NICHE/TOPIC]
>
> Give me 4 of each type:
> 1. **Pattern Interrupt**: Something unexpected or contrarian that stops the scroll
> 2. **Personal Story Lead**: "I once..." or "Last week I..." that hints at a lesson
> 3. **Bold Claim**: A strong, specific statement that demands proof
> 4. **Question Hook**: Not yes/no questions — questions that make people think "Wait, why IS that?"
> 5. **List Tease**: "3 things," "The only 5," etc. — but make the framing unique
>
> For each hook, mark it: [TikTok], [Twitter], [LinkedIn], [Instagram] depending on where it works best.
>
> NO GENERIC HOOKS. Every hook should feel like it was written for MY niche specifically, not a template anyone could use.

**When to use it:** When you're stuck staring at a blank screen or your recent posts are getting ignored in the first 3 seconds.

**Pro tip:** Save the best-performing hooks in a swipe file — great hooks can be remixed dozens of times with different content behind them.

## 6. Content Calendar System

**The Prompt:**

> You are a content strategist building a 30-day posting plan that won't burn me out.
>
> My niche: [NICHE]
>
> Build me a calendar with:
> - 4 content pillars (themes I'll rotate through)
> - Posting frequency: [DAILY / 3X WEEK / 5X WEEK]
> - Primary platform: [PLATFORM]
>
> For each day, give me:
> 1. **Pillar** (which theme)
> 2. **Format** (thread, carousel, video, single post)
> 3. **Topic** (specific, not vague — "How to price your first client" not "Pricing tips")
> 4. **Hook starter** (first line to get me unstuck)
>
> RULES:
> - No back-to-back similar formats (don't post 5 carousels in a row)
> - Mix educational, personal, and conversational content
> - Build in 2-3 "easy days" per week (repost a banger, share a hot take, ask a question — low-effort, high-engagement)
> - One "test" post per week (try a new format or edgy topic to see what happens)

**When to use it:** When you're posting randomly and want a strategic plan that's actually sustainable, not a burnout machine.

**Pro tip:** Batch-create content for one pillar at a time (record 4 videos in one session) rather than switching contexts daily.

## 7. Audience Deep Analysis

**The Prompt:**

> You are an audience research expert who helps creators stop guessing and start knowing their people.
>
> My niche: [NICHE]
>
> Analyze my target audience and give me:
>
> 1. **The Real Problem** — Not what they say they want (e.g., "more followers") but what they actually need (e.g., "validation that their work matters")
> 2. **Language Audit** — 10 phrases, questions, or complaints my audience uses in comments, DMs, or forums. Exact wording, not paraphrased.
> 3. **Content Gaps** — 5 questions they're asking that nobody is answering well (or at all)
> 4. **Emotional Triggers** — What they're afraid of, excited about, frustrated by, and aspiring toward
> 5. **Scroll-Stopping Topics** — 8 content ideas that will make them stop and think "Wait, this is for ME"
>
> Give me insights I can't find in a demographics chart. I need to understand how they think, not just who they are.

**When to use it:** When your content is getting ignored and you suspect you're talking to the wrong pain points or using the wrong language.

**Pro tip:** Run this quarterly and compare results — your audience evolves, and your messaging should evolve with it.

## 8. Viral Content Deconstructor

**The Prompt:**

> You are a content analyst who reverse-engineers why something blew up so I can apply the lessons without copying it.
>
> Analyze this viral post/video: [PASTE URL OR DESCRIBE IT]
>
> Break down:
> 1. **Hook Mechanics** — What specifically made people stop scrolling in the first 2 seconds? (Visuals, words, pattern interrupt?)
> 2. **Emotional Arc** — What emotion did it trigger and when? (Curiosity, anger, validation, surprise?)
> 3. **Story Structure** — Setup, conflict, resolution. Even if it's 15 seconds, there's a structure.
> 4. **Shareability Factor** — Why would someone send this to a friend? What's the "you HAVE to see this" element?
> 5. **Applicability** — How can I use this formula in MY niche without being a copycat?
>
> Then give me 3 remix ideas: ways to apply this viral framework to my content with a unique angle.
>
> Be specific. Not "it had a good hook" — tell me exactly what words, visuals, or techniques made the hook work.

**When to use it:** When you see something blow up in your niche (or adjacent niches) and want to learn from it instead of just envying it.

**Pro tip:** Study viral content outside your niche too — the best hooks and structures often come from unexpected places.

## 9. Comment & DM Response Templates

**The Prompt:**

> You are a community manager who knows that replies are content too — and bad replies kill engagement.
>
> My niche: [NICHE]
>
> Create response templates for:
>
> 1. **Positive comments** (3 variations — don't just say "Thanks!")
> 2. **Questions I get all the time** (5 common questions + responses that give value but tease deeper content)
> 3. **Haters/Skeptics** (3 responses that disarm without being defensive or petty)
> 4. **DMs asking for free advice** (2 templates: one for quick wins, one for redirecting to paid/content)
> 5. **Collaboration requests** (2 templates: interested + not interested, both professional)
>
> RULES:
> - Every response should sound like me, not a chatbot
> - Add value even in short replies (don't just acknowledge — teach or entertain)
> - No "Great question!" or "Thanks for sharing!" — those are filler
> - When redirecting to content/product, make it helpful, not salesy
>
> Give me copy-paste templates that I can personalize in 5 seconds.

**When to use it:** When you're spending too much time on replies or your responses are bland and forgettable.

**Pro tip:** Pin your best replies — a killer response in the comments can become your next post idea or convert lurkers into followers.

## 10. Brand Voice Blueprint

**The Prompt:**

> You are a brand voice architect. Help me define how I sound so I stop second-guessing every post.
>
> My niche: [NICHE]
>
> I want my content to feel: [PICK 2-3: funny, authoritative, rebellious, warm, no-BS, nerdy, cinematic, raw, polished, irreverent, educational]
>
> Build me a voice guide with:
>
> 1. **Voice Principles** (3 rules I follow in every post — e.g., "Always challenge the default advice")
> 2. **Word Bank** — 15 words/phrases I USE and 15 words/phrases I AVOID
> 3. **Sentence Structures** — Show me 5 example sentences that capture my tone
> 4. **Content Personality** — If my brand was a person at a party, who are they and how do they talk?
> 5. **Edge vs. Safety** — Where's my line? What's too far, and what's fair game?
>
> Then rewrite this sample post in MY voice: [PASTE A GENERIC POST FROM YOUR NICHE]
>
> I should read the rewrite and think "YES, that's exactly how I'd say it."

**When to use it:** When your content feels inconsistent, you're copying other people's styles, or you don't have a clear "sound" yet.

**Pro tip:** Reference this document before every content batch — voice drift is real, especially when you're inspired by someone else's content.

## 11. Platform Growth Playbook

**The Prompt:**

> You are a platform growth strategist who builds actionable 90-day plans, not motivational fluff.
>
> Platform: [TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM / TWITTER / LINKEDIN]
> Current followers: [NUMBER]
> Goal: [SPECIFIC GOAL — e.g., "500 followers," "5 clients," "1000 email subs"]
>
> Build me a growth playbook with:
>
> 1. **Core Strategy** — What's the ONE thing I should prioritize? (Algorithm hack, content format, engagement tactic, collaboration strategy?)
> 2. **Daily Actions** (15 min/day max) — Exactly what I do every day to grow. Be specific: "Comment on 5 posts in [niche] using [this framework]."
> 3. **Weekly Experiments** — 3 things to test each week (new format, posting time, content angle, engagement strategy)
> 4. **Growth Triggers** — 5 specific tactics proven to spike growth on this platform right now (not 3 years ago)
> 5. **Avoid These Traps** — 3 common mistakes that kill growth on this platform
>
> Give me a 90-day roadmap: Weeks 1-4 (focus), Weeks 5-8 (expand), Weeks 9-12 (optimize). Make it actionable, not theoretical.

**When to use it:** When you're stuck at a follower plateau, starting from scratch, or don't have a clear growth strategy beyond "post more."

**Pro tip:** Track one metric obsessively each month (engagement rate, profile visits, saves) — vanity metrics lie, behavior metrics don't.

## 12. Content Repurposing Multiplier

**The Prompt:**

> You are a content repurposing expert who turns one piece of content into 10 without it feeling repetitive.
>
> Original content: [PASTE YOUR POST, THREAD, VIDEO SCRIPT, OR ARTICLE]
>
> Transform this into:
>
> 1. **Twitter thread** (6-8 tweets, new hook)
> 2. **Instagram carousel** (5 slides, visual-first approach)
> 3. **LinkedIn post** (personal story angle)
> 4. **TikTok script** (60 seconds, pattern-interrupt hook)
> 5. **Email newsletter section** (conversational, deeper dive)
> 6. **Quote graphic text** (pull 1 punchy sentence that works standalone)
> 7. **Engagement post** (turn the idea into a question or poll)
> 8. **Short-form video concept** (15-sec version for Reels/Shorts)
> 9. **Long-form video outline** (YouTube structure: hook, value, CTA)
> 10. **Follow-up content idea** (what's the next logical post on this topic?)
>
> Each format should feel native to the platform — not like a lazy copy-paste. Change the hook, angle, or framing so it's fresh.

**When to use it:** When you create a banger and want to squeeze every drop of value from it across platforms without sounding redundant.

**Pro tip:** Repurpose your winners, not your flops — if it worked once, it'll work again in a different format or on a different platform.
