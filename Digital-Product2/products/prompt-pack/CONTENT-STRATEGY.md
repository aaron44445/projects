## Demo Strategy (Show Without Giving Away)

### Show the QUESTIONS, not the full prompt
- Film typing a prompt, show AI asking diagnostic questions
- Show 2-3 questions on screen — proves sophistication without revealing engineering

### Show the OUTPUT with key parts blurred
- Screen record ChatGPT's response
- Show structure and depth, blur specific actionable sections
- Highlight one powerful insight

### Show the RESULT in real life
- "Here's what I built from that analysis"
- Show your morning routine, goal tracker, habit system

### Tactical Techniques
- **Partial Reveal:** Show first 20% of response, text: "This goes on for 3 more pages"
- **Question Tease:** "This prompt asks 8 questions. Here are 2 of them:"
- **Comparison:** Generic prompt vs Vault prompt side-by-side
- **Reaction Film:** Record yourself REACTING to output (face, not full screen)
