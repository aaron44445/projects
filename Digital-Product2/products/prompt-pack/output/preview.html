Competitive Landscape Scanner

I'm running [one sentence about your business]. Act as a market intelligence analyst. Map out my competitive landscape: identify my 3 real competitors (not who I think they are — who customers actually choose instead), their core weakness I can exploit, and the one positioning angle that makes me impossible to compare. Be ruthlessly specific. If you need clarification, ask one pointed question.
When to use Before finalizing your marketing message or when competitor research feels overwhelming.
Pro tip The AI often identifies non-obvious competitors (alternatives, not direct clones) — pay attention to those.

Tell me: the single biggest assumption I'm making that's probably wrong, the one cost I'm underestimating, and whether my business has a "rich person problem" or a "real person problem." Decision Clarity Engine

Eisenhower Priority Matrix

You are a time management strategist. Here's what's on my plate:
[Paste your tasks, projects, requests — everything you're juggling]
Sort these into the Eisenhower Matrix and give me:
