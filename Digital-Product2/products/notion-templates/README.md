## How to Use

1. **Purchase** the template pack.
2. **Open** the shared Notion link you receive after purchase.
3. **Click "Duplicate"** in the top-right corner of the Notion page to copy it to your workspace.
4. **Customize** — Change the example data to your own goals, habits, and projects.
5. **Start using** — Each template includes instructions and example entries to get you going.

> **Tip:** You can use the templates independently or together. The Goal Tracker pairs well with the Weekly Review, and the Daily Planner complements the Habit Dashboard.

## Template Details

### 1. Goal Tracker
Set quarterly goals, break them into monthly milestones, then into weekly actions. Progress rolls up automatically — complete your weekly goals and watch your quarterly progress climb.

### 2. Habit Dashboard
Check off habits each day. The template calculates your weekly completion score and tracks streaks automatically. See at a glance which habits are sticking and which need attention.

### 3. Daily Planner
Plan your day in time blocks (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) with priorities (P1/P2/P3) and energy levels. Includes a "New Day" button that sets up your daily template in one click.

### 4. Weekly Review
Every week, answer a short set of reflection prompts: your wins, challenges, lessons, and priorities for next week. Track your energy and productivity scores to spot patterns over months.

### 5. Project Tracker
Manage side projects from idea to shipped. Kanban board for status tracking, timeline for deadlines, and a revenue column so you can see which projects are actually making money.
