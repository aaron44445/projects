# LOCKED IN — Product & Launch Design

**Date:** 2026-03-04
**Status:** Approved

---

## Product Concept

**Name:** LOCKED IN
**Tagline:** "Track everything. Waste nothing."
**Price:** $19.99 via Gumroad/LemonSqueezy
**Platform:** Standalone web app (HTML/CSS/JS), hosted on Vercel, localStorage for data

**Target Buyer:** 18-28 year olds who want to get their life together — money, fitness, habits, goals — but keep failing to start. They consume self-improvement content and believe a system will unlock their potential. They're buying the feeling of control and a new identity.

**Positioning:** Not a planner. Not a to-do list. A personal command center that makes you feel like you're running your life like a CEO. The aesthetic IS the product. Dark theme, gold accents, clean typography, satisfying interactions.

---

## Product Features

### 4 Views via Top Navigation

**1. DASHBOARD (Home)**
- Welcome banner with user's name + rotating motivational quote
- Life Score (0-100) — auto-calculated from habit completion across all categories. Big, animated, center stage. The dopamine hook.
- 4 category cards with quick stats: Money, Body, Mind, Habits
- Weekly snapshot bar chart (past 7 days completion)
- Current streak counter (consecutive days with 80%+ completion)

**2. TRACKER**
- Daily habit check-off grid, organized by category:
  - Money: side hustle work, learning, networking
  - Body: workout, sleep, nutrition
  - Mind: reading, deep work, no doomscroll
  - Spirit: journal, gratitude, faith
- Monthly grid with checkmarks and number inputs
- Per-habit stats: completion %, streak, total
- Month selector pills

**3. GOALS**
- Quarterly goal setting across 4 categories
- Status dropdowns: Not Started / In Progress / Done
- Visual progress indicators
- Target dates

**4. FINANCES**
- Monthly income vs expenses
- Savings goal with progress bar
- Net worth tracker
- Side hustle / income stream tracking

### Setup Flow (First Open)
- Enter name, pick categories, set 1-3 goals
- 60 seconds to complete
- Immediately drops into personalized dashboard

### Technical
- Single HTML file or small static site
- No backend — all data in localStorage
- Responsive (mobile + desktop)
- Deployed on Vercel

## Instagram Strategy

### Account
- Handle: @lockedin.system (or similar available handle)
- Bio: "The system that changed everything. | Track money. Body. Mind. Habits. | Link below"
- Profile pic: Minimal "LI" monogram, gold on black

### Content Pillars

**Product Demos (40%)** — Screen recordings of the app with cinematic music and POV captions. Slow zooms, smooth scrolling, lo-fi or dark cinematic beats. The UI is the star.

**Motivational/Identity (40%)** — Text-over-video, talking head, carousels. Build the brand identity. "You don't need another plan. You need a system." / "Day 30 of tracking everything."

**Results/Social Proof (20%)** — Own tracking screenshots, DM testimonials, before/after dashboards.

### Posting Cadence
- 1-2 Reels daily (non-negotiable for growth)
- 3-4 Stories daily
- 1 Carousel per week
- Batch record 7-10 Reels per session
- Repurpose to TikTok + YouTube Shorts

### Growth Tactics
- 20-30 relevant hashtags per post
- 30 min/day engaging in self-improvement niche comments
- Collaborate with small productivity accounts
- Cross-post to TikTok and YouTube Shorts

## Execution Roadmap

### Phase 1: MVP + Start Content (Days 1-3)
- Day 1: Build core app (dashboard, tracker, goals, finances). Get it looking cinematic.
- Day 1 evening: Create Instagram. Record first 3-5 teaser Reels.
- Days 2-3: Post daily. Keep building between posts.

### Phase 2: Polish + Hype (Days 4-7)
- Days 4-5: Finish all features, onboarding, animations, polish.
- Days 5-6: Set up Gumroad/LemonSqueezy, landing page, checkout.
- Day 7: Deploy final version. Record launch content batch.
- Continue 1-2 Reels daily. Target: 200-500 followers before launch.

### Phase 3: Launch (Days 7-10)
- Drop the product link. Announce in Reels + Stories.
- Post full product demo Reels.
- Target: First sales within 48 hours.

### Phase 4: Grind to 10K (Days 10-180)
- 1-2 Reels daily, weekly content batches
- Repurpose to TikTok + YouTube Shorts
- Engage 30 min/day in niche
- Analyze performance, iterate on winning formats
- V2 features + price bump to $24.99 around month 3

### Milestones
| Milestone | Target | Revenue |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| First sale | Week 2 | $20 |
| 1,000 followers | Month 1 | $200-400 |
| 5,000 followers | Month 3 | $1,000-2,000 |
| 10,000 followers | Month 6 | $10,000+ |
